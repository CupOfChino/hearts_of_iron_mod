[h1]世界线简介[/h1]

欧洲的君主制反动势力重现天日，为成为世界霸主，组建了一个恐怖的军事集团。在外敌的威胁下，帝国主义列强也共同组建军事集团，抗衡欧洲出现的庞然大物。整个世界都处于战火的阴霾之下。

表面团结的各国均暗怀鬼胎，一旦共同的威胁消失，那么他们互相就会成为彼此最大的威胁。最后的赢家永远只会有一个……

该模组目前只是个人制作的半成品，各方面内容都等待补充和打磨。

[h1]模组介绍[/h1]

[h2]基础说明 [/h2]
基于原版游戏，对一些比较幽默和坐牢的倒车线进行修改加强，可作基础模组游玩。文案与翻译大多来自 Deepseek，技术有限敬请包涵。

[h2]主要改动 [/h2]
[list]
[*][b]国家[/b]：德国 / 匈牙利 / 法国国策大幅优化加强，英美法获得专属三军动态民族精神，苏德专属民族精神强化
[*][b]AI[/b]：重点生产中坦与飞机，编制优化为 35/36 宽并提高装甲师倾向
[*][b]建筑[/b]：最多 20 民工造建筑，整合建筑拓展模组（炼钢厂、煤炭工业基地等），部分建筑等级上限翻倍
[*][b]其他[/b]：1000 三军经验上限、150 海军槽、大量消耗政治点决议、工业电子科技微调
[/list]

[h2]新世界线玩法 [/h2]
德国复辟帝制、匈牙利重建奥匈、法国波拿马王朝后，德法奥匈组成《三皇同盟》，英美苏组成《三国协约》，两大集团约在 1941 年开战，战场将涉及整个欧、非、亚、美洲，双方各出动近千师。推荐按德→重建帝制、匈→奥匈、法→波拿马、苏→史实、英→非红中立、美→史实来游玩。

[h3]难度提示 [/h3]
同盟侧较易但吃海军；协约侧较难，陆战压力大（苏联极难被击败）。整体难度偏高，个人测试可能存在 bug，请见谅。

[h2]兼容性与后续计划 [/h2]
[list]
[*]本模组可作为基底游玩，若其他模组国策 / 决议出现 bug，将其移动到本模组上方即可解决
[*]后续计划：新增炼狱难度、二战任务链
[/list]

[h1]Worldline Overview[/h1]

The reactionary monarchist forces of Europe have resurfaced. To dominate the world, they have formed a terrifying military bloc. Under the threat of this external foe, the imperialist powers also banded together into a rival military bloc to counter the behemoth rising in Europe. The whole world now lies under the shadow of war.

Despite their apparent unity, every nation harbours its own agenda. Once the common threat vanishes, they will become each other's greatest danger. There can be only one final victor...

This mod is currently an unfinished work made by a single person; much content still awaits completion and polish.

[h1]Mod Introduction[/h1]

[h2]Basics [/h2]
Built on the vanilla game, this mod revamps and enhances some of the more humorous and painful "restoration" paths. Usable as a base mod. Most text and translations come from Deepseek; technical skill is limited, your patience is appreciated.

[h2]Major Changes [/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Nations[/b]: Germany / Hungary / France focus trees optimized and strengthened; UK/USA/France get a unique tri-service dynamic national spirit; Germany & USSR national spirits enhanced
[*][b]AI[/b]: Prioritizes medium tanks and aircraft; templates optimized to 35/36 width with higher tendency toward armour divisions
[*][b]Buildings[/b]: Up to 20 civilian factories assigned to construction; building expansion mod integrated (steel mills, coal bases, etc.); some building level caps doubled
[*][b]Other[/b]: 1000 XP cap for all services, 150 naval production slots, many decisions to spend political power, industrial/electronic tech tweaks
[/list]

[h2]New Timeline [/h2]
With Germany restoring the monarchy, Hungary rebuilding Austria-Hungary, and France restoring the Bonapartes, the three form the "Three Emperors' Alliance" against the "Triple Entente" (UK/USA/USSR). The clash begins around 1941 across Europe, Africa, Asia and the Americas, with nearly a thousand divisions per side. Recommended setup: Germany → monarchy, Hungary → Austria-Hungary, France → Bonaparte, USSR → historical, UK → neutral non-communist, USA → historical.

[h3]Difficulty [/h3]
The Alliance side is easier but requires naval skill; the Entente side is harder with heavy land pressure (the USSR is extremely hard to capitulate). Overall difficulty is on the high side and, being single-tester, may contain bugs.

[h2]Compatibility & Roadmap [/h2]
[list]
[*]Can be used as a base mod. If other mods' focuses/decisions glitch, move them above this mod in the load order.
[*]Planned: new "Inferno" difficulty and a WWII mission chain.
[/list]
