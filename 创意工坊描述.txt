Three Emperors' Alliance (Original Name: Vanilla Alternate History Path Optimization)

Based on the vanilla game, this mod modifies and enhances some of the more humorous and painful "reverse" (restoration) paths.

Most of the text and translations come from Deepseek.

Limited technical skills.

[h1] Current Mod Version: 1.2.2 [/h1]

Current update content:

Adapted for the new game version.

For more detailed changes, please check the changelog. Minor updates will only be recorded there.

==============================================================================

[h1] Major Changes [/h1]
[b] Nations [/b]

Germany (Focus times reduced; some focus effects adjusted; buffs enhanced; a few new focuses added)

Hungary (Focus times reduced; some focus effects adjusted; buffs enhanced; additional focuses added)

France (Focus times reduced; military and industrial trees heavily reworked while retaining the vanilla basis; most focus effects strengthened; Napoleonic path receives more focuses; buffs enhanced; mutually exclusive focuses removed)

UK, USA, and France each get a unique tri-service dynamic national spirit.

Germany and the USSR get enhanced unique national spirits.

[b] AI Adjustments [/b]

AI focus selection adjusted for countries with modified focus trees.

AI production lines optimized – they will prioritise medium tanks and aircraft.

AI division templates optimised; final templates are 35/36 width, with a higher tendency to use armour divisions.

[b] Final templates tend toward 8 motorised + 8 medium tanks + 2 medium tank destroyers. [/b]

[b] German AI additionally has a tendency toward 8 motorised + 8 heavy tanks + 2 medium tank destroyers. [/b]

[b] Overall difficulty is on the high side, but not as tough as dedicated AI‑enhancement mods. [/b]
[h2] Since this is only tested by one person, difficulty may well have bugs. [/h2]
[h3] If you find it too hard, a starter gift pack is available at game start. [/h3]

[b] Building Adjustments [/b]

Up to 20 civilian factories can be assigned to construct buildings.

Infrastructure, civilian factories, military factories, etc., gain additional effects, but construction cost increases per level.

Integrated building expansion mod – you can build steel mills, coal industrial bases, and other structures.

Some building level caps are doubled (air bases 10 → 20, naval bases 10 → 20).

[b] Other Adjustments [/b]

Experience cap for all three services raised to 1000; navy production now has 150 slots.

Many new decisions added to spend excess political power.

Minor tweaks to industrial/electronic tech tree values; tech gaps between electronic techs widened.

If I missed anything, it's because I forgot.

====================================================================================

[b] This mod can be used as a base mod; compatibility has been tested and is generally fine. [/b]
[b] If you encounter bugs with other mods' focuses or decisions (e.g., Japanese Communist path), you can fix them by moving those mods above this one in the load order. [/b]

[h1] New Timeline: The Three Emperors' Alliance [/h1]

Prerequisites:

Germany restores the monarchy.

Hungary restores Austria‑Hungary.

France restores the Bonaparte dynasty (recommended: enable AI historical focuses; strongly recommend Bulgaria historical, and UK non‑communist).

(Do not play as any Commonwealth nation other than the UK – a decision will forcibly merge them into the UK.)

Content:
Germany, France, and Austria‑Hungary form the "Three Emperors' Alliance," while the UK, USA, and USSR form the "Triple Entente." The two blocs will clash around 1941.
The war will span almost all of Europe, Africa, Asia, and the Americas.
Both sides will field nearly a thousand divisions.
The Alliance side is relatively easier but requires naval proficiency.
The Entente side is harder (less work done) – massive land pressure, especially for the USSR, though the USSR is also very hard to capitulate.

[b] Recommended Paths [/b]

Select "Three Emperors' Alliance" and enable historical focuses.

Germany → Restore Monarchy

Hungary → Austria‑Hungary

France → Bonaparte

USSR → Historical

UK → Non‑communist (best if neutral; avoid civil war – the "Blue" path will mess with guarantees and derail the scenario)

USA → Historical

[h1] 新世界线：三皇同盟 [/h1] 

前提：德国复辟帝制；匈牙利重建奥匈帝国；法国波拿马王朝（建议：勾选ai史实国策；强烈建议保加利亚史实线，英国非红线）
